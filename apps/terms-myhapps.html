Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to MyHapps. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the MyHapps application.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account Information: We collect your email address when you sign in using Google Authentication to save your wallet balance and premium status securely via Firebase.
  • Local Storage: Apps you create or download are stored locally on your device using SQLite. We do not upload your personal app code to our servers unless you explicitly publish it to the Discover marketplace.
  • Device Permissions: Some mini-apps you run within MyHapps may request Camera, Microphone, or Location access. You will always be prompted by the Android OS before these permissions are granted.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following trusted services to operate our app:

  • Google Firebase: For secure login authentication and storing marketplace apps.
  • Google AdMob: To serve advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers to personalize ads.
  • Google Drive API: If you choose to backup your apps, data is saved directly to your personal Google Drive. We cannot access your Drive files.

4. Data Security & Deletion

We take security seriously. Your authentication tokens are handled securely by Google. If you wish to delete your account and associated data, you can do so by contacting us or using the in-app tools (if available).

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at themyhappslabs@zohomail.in.
