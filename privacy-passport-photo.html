Legal · Simple Passport Photo Maker

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

Simple Passport Photo Maker does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information or photos.

All image processing — cropping, enhancing, generating the print sheet — happens entirely on your device. MyHapps Labs has no access to any photos you take or select within the app.

2. Camera & Storage Permissions

The app requests the following Android permissions, used strictly for these purposes:

  • CAMERA — To let you take a new photo inside the app. Photos are never sent anywhere.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — To let you select an existing photo from your gallery.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — To save the finished passport photo sheet to your device's Downloads or Gallery folder.

These permissions are not used for any other purpose.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

This app displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service operated by Google LLC.

Google AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. This data collection is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service — not by MyHapps Labs.

MyHapps Labs does not share any personal data with Google AdMob beyond what is automatically provided by the Android operating system for ad serving.

Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Storage & Security

All images you process using this app are saved locally on your device only. No image data, crop data, or output files are transmitted to any server operated or controlled by MyHapps Labs.

We do not operate any backend servers that receive data from this app.

5. Third-Party Services

Beyond Google AdMob (described above), this app does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, crash reporting, or social login services.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app contains advertisements which may not be appropriate for very young children.

If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us immediately at the address below.

7. Your Rights

Since MyHapps Labs does not collect any personal data through this app, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. All data (photos and outputs) exist only on your own device and can be deleted by you at any time.
